// WHO WE ARE

FROM ENGINEERING ROOTS TO INDUSTRIAL CAPABILITY.

ElectroMech Automation & Engineering Ltd. and ElectroMech Power Ltd. operate across technology, engineering, construction, power, factory automation and energy-related activities. The company profile describes a long-term commitment to professional service, quality, customer satisfaction and continuous development.

ElectroMech's documented journey began in 2007 as an engineering consultancy serving the Textile & RMG industries. Over time, the company expanded into electrical equipment, manufacturing, automation, power systems, testing, transformer-related capabilities and EPC activities.
