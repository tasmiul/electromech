ABOUT ELECTROMECH

Electrical Engineering, Power & Automation Solutions

ElectroMech Automation & Engineering Ltd. and ElectroMech Power Ltd. provide electrical engineering, power distribution, transformer, switchgear, automation, manufacturing, and EPC solutions in Bangladesh. The company began as an engineering consultancy serving the Textile and RMG industries and has since expanded into electrical equipment manufacturing, power systems, factory automation, testing, and engineering services.

01 / COMPANY HISTORY

OUR HISTORICAL JOURNEY SINCE 2007

From an engineering consultancy serving the Textile & RMG industries to an established electrical engineering, manufacturing, power and automation company.

verified13 Documented Milestones
2024MAJOR MILESTONE
PWD Schedule • BSTI Manufacturing License

TRANSFORMER MANUFACTURING & PWD RECOGNITION

ElectroMech transformers were enlisted in the PWD Schedule, and the company obtained its Transformer Manufacturing License from BSTI.

PWD EnlistmentBSTI License
2023ELB APPROVAL • BUET TESTS

TRANSFORMER MANUFACTURING & BUET TESTING

Top management of ELB visited ElectroMech's factory and confirmed their satisfaction with the testing and verification process. ELB approved ElectroMech as an authorized Transformer Manufacturing company. ElectroMech's 11/0.415kV, 2000kVA Transformer, 3200A LT Switchgear and 1200kVAR PFI Plant passed BUET tests.

2022CPRI • BREB • HEAD OFFICE

TESTING, POWER SYNCHRONIZATION & HEAD OFFICE

ElectroMech's transformers passed CPRI tests. The company successfully synchronized a gas generator with the BREB source using a 33/11kV, 5MVA transformer. ElectroMech's Head Office was positioned at its own space at Wahid Masafi Centre, 73 Purana Paltan Line, Dhaka.

2021MITSUBISHI ASC • POWDER COATING

AUTHORIZED SERVICE CENTRE & MANUFACTURING DEVELOPMENT

ElectroMech was further recognized by its international partner Mitsubishi Electric as an Authorized Service Centre (ASC) for MELCO products. The company also developed a Sheet Treatment Process and Powder Coating Plant at its own premises.

2020MITSUBISHI ELECTRIC

MITSUBISHI AUTHORIZED SERVICE CENTRE

ElectroMech achieved Mitsubishi Electric Authorized Service Centre recognition, strengthening its service and technical support capabilities.

201933kV POWER SYSTEM

33kV SYSTEM COMPLETION

ElectroMech successfully completed a 33kV system with HT, LT and power transformers using its own resources and own factory.

2018FACTORY AUTOMATION • 100kV LAB

FACTORY AUTOMATION & HIGH-VOLTAGE TESTING

ElectroMech introduced factory automation into its manufacturing process and installed automatic slitting, bending, busbar processing and multifunction machines. The company also developed its own laboratory for high-voltage testing up to 100kV, including Load/No Load Loss, Oil Insulation, Winding Resistance, Double Voltage and Double Frequency tests.

2016ELECTRICAL LICENSING BOARD

ELB RECOGNITION

ElectroMech received recognition from the Electrical Licensing Board (ELB).

201525,000 SQ. FT. FACTORY

FACTORY & MANUFACTURING EXPANSION

ElectroMech developed a complete 25,000 sq. ft. factory on its own land and started manufacturing panel systems from 11kV to machine connections, distribution transformers and related equipment.

2012MITSUBISHI ELECTRIC

MITSUBISHI AUTHORIZED MARKETING NETWORK

Mitsubishi authorized ElectroMech to extend its marketing network throughout Bangladesh for complete electrical solutions using Mitsubishi's range of products.

2011MITSUBISHI ELECTRIC • JAPAN

BUSINESS COLLABORATION

ElectroMech established a business collaboration with Mitsubishi Electric Co., Japan, for low-voltage equipment.

2010COMPANY FORMATION • 2,000 SQ. FT.

COMPANY FORMATION & ENGINEERING WORKSHOP

ElectroMech Automation & Engineering Ltd. was formed and registered with the Registrar of Joint Stock Companies of Bangladesh as a Limited Company. The company also established a 2,000 sq. ft. engineering workshop and started fabrication of HT, LT, PFI Plant and MCC electrical panels.

2007Engineering Consultancy

THE BEGINNING

ElectroMech began as an engineering consultant for Textile & RMG industries under Electromech Engineering Services. Its valuable services led satisfied clients to invite the company to offer products and services alongside consulting.
